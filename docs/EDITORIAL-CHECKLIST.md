# Pre-publication editorial checklist

Run through this before clicking **Publish** on any article. It reflects the standards in the [editorial policy](../src/content/pages/editorial-policy.md).

## Accuracy and sourcing

- [ ] Every factual claim (specifications, prices, dates, figures, statements by companies) is backed by a reliable source, and primary sources are preferred.
- [ ] Key sources are listed under **Sources and references**, and links have been opened and checked.
- [ ] No quotation, benchmark result, test experience, price, release date or statistic has been invented or "filled in".
- [ ] Prices are described as price bands, or dated ("at the time of writing").
- [ ] Facts, analysis, opinion and estimates are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] Anything that couldn't be verified is either removed or explicitly labelled as unverified.

## Reviews and rankings

- [ ] The review says honestly whether it is **hands-on** or **research-based** (the **Used hands-on?** switch matches reality).
- [ ] A **Score** is given only for hands-on reviews, following [How we review](../src/content/pages/how-we-review.md).
- [ ] Strengths and weaknesses are both covered fairly.
- [ ] Ranked lists explain their selection criteria and why the order is justified.
- [ ] Alternatives are mentioned where they help readers.

## Crypto and financial topics

- [ ] No investment advice, price predictions or performance promises.
- [ ] Risks are stated plainly.

## AI assistance

- [ ] A person has read and checked the whole article. Nothing AI-generated is published without review.
- [ ] Facts suggested by AI tools have been verified against primary sources.
- [ ] AI-generated images are marked **Image is AI-generated** and never presented as real photographs.

## Commercial relationships

- [ ] Affiliate links come only from programmes actually joined, and are added through **Affiliate links** or marked with the `"affiliate"` title.
- [ ] Sponsored content has **Sponsored content** switched on and the sponsor named.
- [ ] No commercial relationship has influenced the verdict.

## Presentation

- [ ] The title is clear and specific; the excerpt summarises the article in one or two sentences.
- [ ] The URL slug is short, descriptive and final.
- [ ] The category is correct.
- [ ] Headings are structured (H2 for sections, H3 for subsections).
- [ ] The featured image has meaningful **alt text** and a credit where needed.
- [ ] Tables are used only for genuinely tabular information.
- [ ] Internal links point to related TekkTech articles.
- [ ] Length suits the subject (typically 600–1,000 words).
- [ ] Spelling and grammar have been checked. The preview pane looks right.
- [ ] Optional: the SEO title (70 characters or fewer) and SEO description (120–160 characters) are written for search results.

## After publishing

- [ ] The Cloudflare deployment succeeded.
- [ ] The live article looks correct on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] It appears in its category page and in search.
