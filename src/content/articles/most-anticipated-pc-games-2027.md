PC gamers have plenty to look forward to beyond the current release calendar, but predicting the biggest year in gaming is never straightforward. Announcements, delays and changes in development plans can quickly reshape which games are expected to arrive and when.

Looking towards 2027, the most interesting prospects span established franchises, role-playing games, open-world adventures and new projects from recognisable studios. Some have public release windows, while others remain anticipated titles without a firm date.

That distinction matters. A game announced for a particular year is not guaranteed to launch on schedule, and a game coming to consoles is not automatically confirmed for PC.

The following selection focuses on announced or publicly listed PC releases worth watching. It is an editorial shortlist, not a definitive ranking of which games will be best.

## 1. Fable

The return of Fable is one of the more interesting prospects for PC players who enjoy fantasy role-playing games.

The series is known for its distinctive British humour, fantasy setting and emphasis on player choice. A new entry offers an opportunity to revisit that identity with modern graphics and contemporary open-world design.

The biggest questions concern how the new game balances exploration, combat, character development and the choices that make role-playing games feel personal.

For PC players, performance and graphics options will also matter. Large environments and detailed visual effects can create challenges across a wide range of hardware.

Fable is worth following because it combines a recognisable franchise with the possibility of a fresh start. Check the latest official announcements for its release window and PC availability before making plans.

## 2. Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis is another major name to watch, particularly for players interested in exploration, environmental puzzles and cinematic adventure.

The Tomb Raider series has repeatedly reinterpreted its central character and gameplay, combining traversal, ancient locations, puzzle-solving and action.

The appeal of a new entry lies in how it develops those foundations. Interesting environments and well-designed puzzles can be just as important as combat or graphical detail.

PC players should also look for information about control options, ultrawide support, graphics settings and accessibility features as launch approaches.

The game is listed among upcoming releases, but release timing and final technical details should be checked against the latest publisher announcements.

## 3. Stranger Than Heaven

Stranger Than Heaven, from RGG Studio, is worth watching for players interested in character-driven action and crime drama.

The project has drawn attention for its historical setting and presentation, which differ from the contemporary urban environments associated with much of the studio's best-known work.

Its potential appeal comes from the combination of a distinctive setting, cinematic storytelling and action-focused gameplay.

However, details about the final PC version, performance and the full scope of its systems should not be assumed before the developer confirms them.

## 4. Ananta

Ananta is an ambitious-looking project built around an urban setting, character abilities and a broad range of activities.

Its early presentation has attracted attention because of its mixture of open-world exploration, traversal and action. That combination could appeal to PC players who enjoy games where movement through the environment is as important as combat.

The challenge will be delivering a cohesive experience rather than simply a large collection of activities.

Because development plans can change, check the latest official information for its launch timing, monetisation model and PC requirements.

## 5. Persona 4 Revival

Persona 4 Revival is a title worth monitoring for fans of Japanese role-playing games and character-focused storytelling.

The original Persona 4 is known for its combination of supernatural mystery, social simulation and turn-based combat. A revival provides an opportunity to introduce that experience to a new audience while revisiting its characters and setting.

The key questions are how closely the new version follows the original, what technical or gameplay changes are introduced and whether the PC release offers useful modern features.

Until further details are confirmed, avoid assuming a particular release date or the precise extent of any changes.

## 6. The Wolf Among Us 2

The Wolf Among Us 2 remains interesting to players who enjoy narrative adventures and branching conversations.

The original game developed a following through its detective-story structure, dark fairy-tale setting and character-driven decisions.

A sequel has the potential to revisit those strengths while taking advantage of newer hardware and presentation techniques. However, long development cycles and uncertain release plans make it particularly important to distinguish an announced project from a game with a confirmed launch date.

Treat it as a title to watch rather than a guaranteed 2027 release until the developer provides clear confirmation.

## 7. Marvel's Blade

Marvel's Blade is an appealing prospect for players interested in action games based on established comic-book characters.

Blade's supernatural setting creates opportunities for a darker atmosphere, close-quarters combat and distinctive enemy design.

The main question is how the developers translate the character into a satisfying game rather than relying on the appeal of the licence alone.

Its final gameplay, technical requirements and PC launch timing need to be confirmed through official updates.

## 8. Clockwork Revolution

Clockwork Revolution is another announced project worth following, particularly for players who enjoy science-fiction settings, role-playing systems and games built around consequential choices.

Its steampunk-inspired world offers a distinctive visual direction, while its role-playing ambitions could appeal to fans of systems-driven adventures.

The important unknowns are how its mechanics fit together, how much freedom players will have and how the final game balances narrative and exploration.

As with other long-awaited projects, its appearance on a future-release list should not be treated as proof of a confirmed 2027 launch.

## 9. The Expanse: Osiris Reborn

The Expanse: Osiris Reborn is worth watching for fans of science-fiction role-playing games and the broader universe of The Expanse.

The setting provides opportunities for political tension, space-based environments and character-driven decisions. Translating that universe into a compelling interactive experience will depend on the quality of its combat, exploration and storytelling.

PC players should look for more details on the role-playing systems, hardware requirements and how closely the experience follows the source material.

Its eventual release timing should be checked against current developer announcements.

## 10. Kingdom Hearts IV

Kingdom Hearts IV is a major prospect for fans of action role-playing games and the long-running crossover series.

The franchise combines fast-paced combat, fantasy storytelling and recognisable characters from multiple entertainment properties. A new entry could offer a substantial visual and mechanical evolution.

The biggest challenge is balancing accessibility for new players with the continuity expected by long-time fans.

A PC release and launch window should not be assumed without official confirmation. Follow publisher announcements for reliable information about platforms and timing.

## How to interpret the 2027 release calendar

Upcoming game lists frequently combine several different kinds of information:

- **Confirmed dates:** A publisher has announced a specific launch date.
- **Release windows:** A game is expected within a month, quarter or year.
- **Undated announcements:** The project is public, but a launch date has not been confirmed.
- **Speculation:** A title is expected by commentators or fans without a firm official commitment.

These categories should not be treated as equivalent. Development schedules change, and PC releases can follow console launches rather than arrive simultaneously.

If you are planning purchases or upgrading a gaming PC, wait for confirmed system requirements and reliable performance information before spending money based on a particular game's anticipated arrival.

## The bottom line

The most anticipated PC games of 2027 cover a broad range of genres, from fantasy role-playing and cinematic adventure to science fiction and narrative-driven experiences.

Fable and Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis are particularly worth watching, while Stranger Than Heaven, Ananta and Persona 4 Revival offer different reasons to be interested in the year ahead.

However, an exciting announcement is not the same as a confirmed launch. Several projects remain subject to changing schedules, and their final PC performance and requirements are not yet established.

Keep an eye on official developer announcements, treat undated projects cautiously and revisit the release calendar as new information becomes available. That is the best way to separate genuine 2027 releases from games that may arrive later.
