Wireless earbuds have reached the point where almost every premium pair sounds good and blocks out a fair amount of noise. The real differences now lie in fit, battery life, how well they work with your phone, and whether the extra features are worth paying for. This ranking is based on manufacturer specifications and published reviews from established outlets. TekkTech has not tested these earbuds hands-on.

## How we chose

We focused on earbuds that are currently on sale and widely reviewed, and that represent clear choices for different buyers. We weighed:

- **Sound and noise cancelling**, based on the consensus of published reviews
- **Battery life**, using manufacturer figures
- **Ecosystem fit**, because some features only work with certain phones
- **Value**, since an older or cheaper pair can be the smarter buy

Prices below are UK launch or list prices unless stated. Earbud prices move often, so check current deals before buying.

## 1. Sony WF-1000XM6: best overall

Sony's flagship earbuds launched in February 2026 and have been widely praised as the most complete all-rounder available. What Hi-Fi? now ranks them as its best overall wireless earbuds.

Key points:

- A new QN3e noise-cancelling processor, which Sony says is three times faster than the one in the previous model
- Eight microphones in each earbud
- LDAC support for higher-quality Bluetooth audio on compatible Android phones
- Wireless charging case and IPX4 water resistance

**The catch:** battery life is unchanged from the previous generation. Reviewers have also noted a bulky case and limited eartip options. In the US the price rose to $329.99, though the UK price stayed at £250.

## 2. Apple AirPods Pro 3: best for iPhone users

If you use an iPhone, the AirPods Pro 3 are hard to ignore. Seamless pairing, automatic switching between Apple devices and spatial audio all work best within Apple's ecosystem.

What is new this generation:

- Apple claims up to twice the noise cancellation of the AirPods Pro 2
- A built-in heart-rate sensor for workouts, which can track up to 50 workout types
- Live Translation, which needs an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone running iOS 26
- IP57 dust and water resistance

**The catch:** earbud battery life rose to eight hours, but total battery life with the case fell from 30 hours to 24. TechRadar also measured noticeably less than Apple's claim in its own testing. On Android, many of the headline features are unavailable.

## 3. Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen): best for noise cancelling

Bose has long been the name to beat for blocking out noise, and several reviewers single out the second-generation QC Ultra Earbuds for it. They also stand out for comfort, thanks to stability bands that help them stay in place.

- Bose's CustomTune technology tunes the sound to your ears
- The case now supports wireless charging, which the first generation lacked
- Three eartip sizes and three stability band sizes in the box

**The catch:** six hours per charge is the shortest in this list. They were also the most expensive at launch, though Bose UK has since listed them at £249.95.

## 4. Samsung Galaxy Buds 4 Pro: best for Samsung phone owners

Samsung announced the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro in February 2026 alongside the Galaxy S26. Like AirPods with iPhones, they make the most sense if you already own a Samsung phone. Some features, including 24-bit Hi-Fi sound, require a compatible Galaxy device.

- Larger earbud batteries than the previous model
- Samsung-exclusive features, including Galaxy AI integration

**The catch:** reports indicate battery life with noise cancelling on is still around six hours per charge. On a non-Samsung Android phone or an iPhone, much of their advantage disappears.

## 5. Sony WF-C710N: best value

You do not need to spend £200 or more to get good noise cancelling. The WF-C710N won a What Hi-Fi? award in 2025 and are regularly recommended as one of the best budget options.

- Active noise cancelling at around £100
- 8.5 hours per charge with noise cancelling on, and 30 hours in total
- Comfortable fit and an app with EQ adjustment

**The catch:** there is no LDAC or aptX support, only SBC and AAC. Noise cancelling is simply on or off, and the build feels less premium than the flagships. They are often discounted below list price.

## Also worth considering

If sound quality matters more to you than features, reviewers frequently recommend the **Technics EAH-AZ100** and the **Sennheiser Momentum True Wireless** range. The older **Sony WF-1000XM5** also remains a strong choice if you find it at a good discount.

## The bottom line

For most people, the **Sony WF-1000XM6** are the safest premium choice, offering excellent sound, strong noise cancelling and broad compatibility. If you are committed to an iPhone, the **AirPods Pro 3** are the more sensible pick. If you own a Samsung phone, the **Galaxy Buds 4 Pro** make the same case. Choose the **Bose QC Ultra Earbuds** if silence and comfort matter most. If you want good earbuds without a premium price, the **Sony WF-C710N** deliver far more than their price suggests. Whichever you choose, wait for a sale if you can, as premium earbuds are discounted often.
