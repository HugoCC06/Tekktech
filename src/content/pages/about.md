TekkTech is an independent technology publication for people who care about the devices, software and networks that shape everyday life. We publish reviews, comparisons, ranked buying guides, explainers and analysis across four areas:

- **[Consumer Tech](/consumer-tech/)** — phones, laptops, wearables, audio and the gadgets worth your money.
- **[Gaming Tech](/gaming/)** — consoles, PC hardware, monitors and peripherals.
- **[Crypto & Blockchain](/crypto/)** — how blockchains, wallets and digital assets actually work.
- **[Productivity & Software](/productivity/)** — the apps and services that help you get work done.

## What we stand for

**Usefulness over noise.** Every article should help a reader understand something or make a better decision. We would rather publish one genuinely useful guide than five thin ones.

**Honesty about what we know.** We say clearly whether a review is based on hands-on use or on research into published specifications and documentation. We never claim to have tested something we have not, and we never invent quotes, benchmark results, prices or specifications.

**Sources you can check.** Wherever possible we link to primary sources — manufacturer documentation, official announcements, standards bodies and research papers — so you can verify what we write.

**Clear separation of fact and opinion.** Reported facts, analysis and opinion are labelled as such. Our opinion pieces are clearly marked.

**No hype, especially in crypto.** We explain how technology works and what the risks are. We do not give investment advice or make price predictions.

## How TekkTech is funded

TekkTech may earn money through clearly labelled affiliate links and, in future, advertising. Commercial relationships never decide what we cover or what our verdicts say. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) and [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) for details.

## Who writes TekkTech

Articles are published under the TekkTech name. Our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) explains how we research, write, review and correct our work, including how we use AI assistance.
