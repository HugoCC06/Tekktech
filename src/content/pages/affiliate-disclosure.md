TekkTech is reader-supported. Some articles contain **affiliate links**: if you click one and buy something, the retailer may pay TekkTech a small commission. The price you pay is not affected.

## How we label affiliate links

- Articles containing affiliate links show an **affiliate disclosure at the top of the article**, before any links.
- Individual affiliate links are labelled "(affiliate link)", and "Where to buy" boxes are marked as affiliate links.
- Affiliate links are marked with `rel="sponsored nofollow"` so search engines can identify them.

## What affiliate links never do

- They never influence our verdicts, scores or rankings.
- They never decide which products we cover. We regularly recommend products for which we earn nothing.
- Retailers and manufacturers never see or approve our articles before publication.
