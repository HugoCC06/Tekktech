This policy explains the standards every TekkTech article is held to. If you believe we have fallen short of them, please [tell us](/contact/).

## Accuracy and sourcing

- We base factual claims on **primary sources** wherever possible: manufacturer documentation, official announcements, standards bodies, regulatory filings, academic research and technical documentation.
- Articles list their key sources in a **Sources and references** section, and link to them inline where helpful.
- We do **not** invent quotations, product specifications, release dates, benchmark results, testing experiences, prices, market data or sources.
- Where information cannot be independently verified, we say so. We distinguish clearly between **established facts**, **reported claims**, **analysis**, **opinion** and **estimates**.

## Reviews

- Every review states whether it is based on **hands-on use** or on **research** into published specifications, documentation and other sources.
- We only give a numerical score when a product has been assessed hands-on using the method described in [How we review](/how-we-review/).
- We never imply that we physically tested a product when we did not.
- Manufacturers and retailers do not see reviews before publication and cannot approve or change them.

## Crypto and financial topics

Our crypto and blockchain coverage explains technology and risk. It is **not financial or investment advice**. We do not make price predictions, publish fabricated performance figures or promote specific assets.

## Affiliate links and sponsorship

- Some articles contain affiliate links, which may earn TekkTech a commission at no extra cost to you. Articles with affiliate links display a disclosure **before** the content, and the links themselves are labelled.
- Commercial relationships never influence our verdicts, rankings or what we choose to cover.
- Sponsored content, if we ever publish it, will be labelled prominently as sponsored and name the sponsor.
- See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for details.

## Use of AI assistance

TekkTech may use AI tools to help with research, outlining, editing and illustration. We hold ourselves to these rules:

- **No article is published without human review.** A person checks every article for accuracy, sourcing and fairness before it is published.
- AI output is treated as a draft, never as a source. Facts are verified against primary sources.
- Article illustrations may be AI-generated. AI-generated images are labelled as illustrations and are **never presented as photographs** of real products or events.

## Corrections

We correct errors promptly and transparently. When we make a significant correction, we update the article, change its "Updated" date and, where appropriate, add a note explaining what changed. To report an error, use our [contact page](/contact/).

## Independence

TekkTech is independent. No manufacturer, retailer, exchange or advertiser has editorial control over what we publish.
