## The short version

**The public TekkTech website does not set cookies by default.** You can read every article without accepting any cookies.

## Cookies currently in use

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Duration | Who receives it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `__Host-tt_oauth_state` | TekkTech | Security check during the site owner's sign-in to the private publishing dashboard | 10 minutes | Only the site owner, when signing in at /admin/ |

## Local storage

The private publishing dashboard stores the owner's sign-in session in the browser's local storage. It is never used for visitors.

## Advertising

TekkTech does not currently display advertising. If that changes, this policy will be updated and, where required, we will ask for your consent before any advertising cookies are set.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not affect your ability to read TekkTech.
